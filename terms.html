Section 02

About the App

Scriptly is an AI-powered note-taking application for iOS and Android that includes the following features:

  • Real-Time Speech to Text — Transcribe live speech as you talk.
  • AI Filler Word Removal — Automatically remove filler words such as "um", "uh", and "like" from transcriptions.
  • Multi-Language Support — Transcribe speech in dozens of languages.
  • Smart Note Editor — Edit, format, and organize your transcriptions.
  • Folders & Organization — Organize notes and recordings into custom folders.
  • MP3 Transcription — Import MP3 audio files and generate transcriptions.
  • MP4 Video Transcription — Extract and transcribe audio from MP4 video files.
  • PDF & Word Export — Export notes as PDF or .docx Word documents.

All features operate entirely on your device. No audio, transcriptions, or notes are transmitted to external servers.
